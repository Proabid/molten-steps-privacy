Molten Steps Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026. Prepared for app version 0.60.0 and later versions with the same data practices.

This policy covers the Molten Steps application and information you choose to send to its developer for support.

Camera images and your real floor

If you choose the Photograph floor appearance, Molten Steps requests access to the headset's passthrough camera. Camera frames may contain your surroundings and people or belongings visible to the camera. The app uses these frames and camera poses to create a floor texture for the cracking and falling floor effect.

Camera processing happens on the headset. Molten Steps does not save the captured camera frames or the resulting floor photograph as image files, and its game code does not upload them. The floor texture is held in app memory during the current session. It can remain available while you retry rounds or pause the app. It is released when you redraw the area or the app process ends. Selecting a built-in floor style stops use of the photograph but can retain the existing texture in memory until that reset or exit. Putting the headset into standby does not necessarily end that process. Camera capture is disabled when the floor photograph is frozen for gameplay.

You can choose Stone or Concrete to play without photographing your floor. You can deny camera permission or revoke it through your headset's app permissions. To clear a photograph already held in the running app, redraw the area or fully close the app.

Movement and information about your play space

Molten Steps uses the headset and controller positions, orientations and inputs to place the game, detect movement, interact with menus and determine collisions and scores.

The area you draw, excluded areas and any room information you choose to import are used to keep gameplay within your selected space. Molten Steps uses a local spatial anchor to recover alignment after tracking changes or standby. The current area and anchor are used during the app session. Molten Steps does not implement cloud anchor sharing.

Saved courses and Infinity routes contain local positions and dimensions and a room compatibility key. They can therefore describe aspects of your play space. They are saved on the headset when you use the save function.

Local game saves

The app saves settings, movement preferences, selected modes, tutorial state, local records and course completion times on your headset. Saved courses and Infinity routes are also stored locally. Molten Steps does not require a separate developer account, operate an online leaderboard, or automatically send these saves to the developer.

Local diagnostic logs

Molten Steps writes diagnostic files on the headset to help investigate technical problems.

Performance files include timestamps, app/build version, device model, graphics hardware and operating system information, game state, focus and tracking status, frame timing, memory use, floor segment counts, photo coverage percentage and the approximate area of the play space. They do not contain camera images.

Infinity logs include the chosen obstacle types, accepted or rejected placements, placement reasons, distances and progress along the route, visibility and activation events, collisions and points. App/system debug output can also contain diagnostic messages and, when a drawn route is rejected, its route data.

Molten Steps does not automatically upload these logs. The ten most recent Infinity session log files are retained, with older files removed when a new Infinity session starts. Performance files do not currently have an automatic expiration period and remain until the app's local data is removed. System debug logs follow the headset operating system's retention behavior.

Support and information you choose to share

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide. This may include diagnostic logs, screenshots or recordings. Please review attachments and avoid sending private images or unrelated personal information.

We use support information to answer your request, investigate problems and protect the service. The published contact mailbox is hosted by Google. Email and attachments are processed through the email services involved and may be processed in countries outside your own.

We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations. You may ask us to delete information you have sent. Information required by law may need to be retained for the applicable period.

Sharing and platform services

Molten Steps's game code does not automatically transmit camera images, saved courses or diagnostic files to the developer. The app does not include advertising, a developer-operated analytics backend, data selling or cross-app advertising tracking.

Meta provides the headset operating system, store, account, permissions and tracking services. Those services may process data independently under Meta's own terms and privacy policy. The app uses Unity and Meta XR software components. The release configuration disables Unity Analytics, advertising and cloud diagnostics reporting and excludes app internet permission. These statements describe the app configuration and do not override independent processing by the headset platform.

Information you voluntarily send for support is handled as described above. We may also disclose information we actually hold where required by applicable law.

Your choices, access and deletion

You can stop optional camera processing and choose a built-in floor texture. You can also revoke optional permissions using your headset settings.

To remove all Molten Steps saves, settings, saved courses and app-owned diagnostic files from the headset, remove the app's local data using the headset's available storage controls or uninstall Molten Steps. Removing app data resets your records and saved courses. The exact controls depend on the headset software version.

For assistance, access requests or deletion requests, email ettavett@gmail.com with the subject "Molten Steps privacy request". Identify the request and any support conversation or files you previously sent. The developer cannot remotely access or erase files that exist only on your headset.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or receive your personal data, restrict processing, object to certain processing, withdraw consent, and complain to your local data protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Purposes and legal grounds

We process data to provide the mixed reality game and the optional features you select, save your progress, diagnose faults and respond to support requests. Where a legal basis is required, processing necessary to provide the game relies on performance of our agreement with you. Where consent is required for optional processing, including camera access, we rely on that consent. Necessary support, security and fault investigation may rely on our legitimate interests, subject to your rights. We may also process information to comply with legal obligations.

Account and age restrictions

Molten Steps is subject to Meta's account, age and permission restrictions, including restrictions on camera access. A parent or guardian can contact us about information sent to support on behalf of a child. Choosing a built-in floor texture avoids the optional floor photography feature.

Security and changes

Molten Steps uses headset permissions and app-specific local storage to limit access. People with access to an unlocked headset or authorized debugging tools may be able to access local files. No storage or communication system can be guaranteed completely secure.

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change. The updated policy will identify its revision date. Contact Abdulbari Abdlbari at ettavett@gmail.com with questions.
